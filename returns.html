Return & Refund Policy

At Jewels Palace, every piece undergoes careful quality checks before being dispatched. However, if you need to return an eligible product, please review the following policy carefully.

2-Day Return Window

Unused and unworn jewellery may be returned within 2 days of delivery, subject to the conditions mentioned below. To be eligible for a return, the product must be:

  • Unused and unworn
  • In its original condition
  • Free from damage or alteration
  • Returned with its original packaging and components

Quality Inspection

Every returned product undergoes a thorough quality inspection after reaching our facility. A return may be rejected if the product is found to be:

  • Damaged after delivery
  • Worn or excessively used
  • Altered, repaired, or tampered with
  • Missing original components or packaging
  • Replaced or duplicated with another item

Jewels Palace reserves the right to reject a return if the returned product does not match the condition in which it was originally delivered.

How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, customers must submit a return request within 2 days of receiving the order through our website or by contacting us at hello@jewelspalace.com.

Once your request is reviewed, further return instructions will be provided.

Refund Process

Once the returned item reaches our facility:

  • The product will undergo a quality inspection.
  • If the product passes inspection, the return will be approved.
  • The eligible refund will be processed to the original source account/payment method within 7 days.

Please note that the time taken for the refunded amount to appear in your account may additionally depend on your bank or payment provider.

No Exchange Policy

Currently, Jewels Palace does not offer exchanges. Customers are encouraged to carefully review product details, sizing information, and other relevant specifications before placing an order.

Custom & Made-to-Order Jewellery

Customised, engraved, personalised, or specially made-to-order jewellery may not be eligible for return unless the product is received damaged or defective.
