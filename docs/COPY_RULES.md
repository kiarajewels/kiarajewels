# Copy Rules — Kiara Jewels

This file is the single source of truth for all marketing copy, product descriptions, and claims on kiarajewels.co. Every developer and copywriter must check this before publishing text.

## Allowed, accurate wording

- "925 sterling silver" (never "pure silver")
- "Premium CZ (cubic zirconia) stones with diamond-like sparkle"
- "Rhodium-plated with an anti-tarnish coating for long-lasting shine"
- "Made to order by artisans with 10+ years of experience"
- "Free shipping across India"
- "3-day returns"
- For SEO only: "American Diamond" may be used in titles and descriptions, always together with "cubic zirconia (CZ)" so it is clear these are not natural or lab-grown diamonds.

## Banned until confirmed by the owner

Do **not** use any of the following words or phrases anywhere on the site:

- "waterproof"
- "sweatproof"
- "tarnish-free"
- "hypoallergenic"
- "nickel-free"
- "pure" (when referring to silver — use "925 sterling silver" instead)
- "real diamond"
- "certified"
- "lifetime" (as in "lifetime warranty" or "lifetime guarantee")
- "authentic diamond"

### Replacement copy for care/durability

> Everyday wear friendly. Remove before swimming or bathing and keep away from perfume and harsh chemicals to keep the finish lasting longer.

## Never invent

- Reviews, testimonials, or ratings
- Customer counts (e.g. "10,000+ happy customers")
- Press mentions or awards
- Certifications
- Fake stock counters or countdown timers

If we have no real proof, show nothing instead.

## Consistency rules

| Wrong | Correct |
|-------|---------|
| "Free express delivery" | "Free shipping across India" |
| "custom orders ready within 3 days" | "Made to order, about 4 days to make plus about 3 days delivery" |
| "Tarnish Free" | "Rhodium-plated with an anti-tarnish coating" |
| "2-day returns" | "3-day returns" |
| Pieces are "selected" | Pieces are "made in-house, to order" |

## Tagline

"More than jewellery, it's a part of you."

May appear: once on the home page, on the About page, and in the page `<title>`. Never more.

## Brand voice

Short, specific sentences. Refined, warm, honest. Quietly premium, never pushy. Avoid empty filler phrases repeated across sections.
